# Story 9.2: Shared queue across professionals

## Status

Draft

## Story

**As a receptionist,**
**I want** patients who can see any professional in a shared queue,
**so that** whoever is free takes the next patient.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The clinic chooses which professionals share a queue.
2. "Next patient" for any of them takes the top of the shared list.
3. Patients booked with a named professional stay in that professional's queue.
