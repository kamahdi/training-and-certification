# Story 7.3: Log of who viewed form answers

## Status

Draft

## Story

**As a clinic manager,**
**I want** to know who opened each patient's form answers,
**so that** we can answer a patient's or the regulator's access request.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Every opening of a patient's answers records who, when and which patient.
2. The manager can search the log by patient or by staff member.
3. Nobody can edit or delete log entries.
